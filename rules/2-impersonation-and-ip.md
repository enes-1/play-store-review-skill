# 2. Impersonation & Intellectual Property

Apps must not mislead users by imitating another app, developer, brand, or entity. In addition, apps must respect intellectual property rights.

## 2.1 Impersonation

Do not falsely claim association with or authorization from an entity unless explicitly granted.
- **Titles/Icons**: Cannot use names, logos, or icons that resemble another popular app (e.g., using a similar ghost icon to Snapchat).
- **Developer Name**: Cannot use another developer's name to trick users.

### Checklist
- [ ] No logos similar to other popular apps.
- [ ] No misleading text ("From the makers of X" unless true).

## 2.2 Copyright Infringement

Apps must not use copyrighted or protected material without permission.
- **Images/Music/Video**: Ensure you own the rights to all media used in your app.
- **TV/Movies**: Linking to pirated movies or providing unauthorized streaming is an immediate suspension.
- **Game Emulators**: Allowed in certain conditions, but downloading copyrighted ROMs is forbidden.

## 2.3 Trademark Infringement

Do not use trademarks that confuse users about the origin of the product.
- **Fan Apps**: Must explicitly state "Fan App" or "Unofficial" in the title and description (e.g., "Unofficial Guide for GTA" instead of "GTA Guide").

## 2.4 Counterfeit Goods

An app cannot facilitate the sale of counterfeit goods. Selling fake designer bags, electronics, or unauthorized copies will lead to immediate developer account termination.
